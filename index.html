Chapter 2 of 5

How one ant picks the next city

An ant standing at A does not plan the whole trip. It picks the next city at random, but not evenly: close cities and cities with a strong pheromone trail are more likely.

pij = τijα · ηijβΣl τilα · ηilβ with ηij = 1 / dij

τ is the pheromone on the edge, η is how close the city is. α and β say how much the ant trusts each one.

The ant draws a random number r between 0 and 1 and goes where it lands.

Chapter 3 of 5

A colony builds tours

Now three ants start at A, C and E. At every move each ant uses the rule from chapter 2 to pick a city it has not visited yet. After the last city it walks home.

Chapter 4 of 5

Trails fade, good tours get stronger

After the round, the pheromone changes in two steps. First every trail evaporates a little. Then each ant deposits pheromone on the edges it used: Q / L, so a shorter tour leaves more.

τij ← (1 − ρ) · τij + Σk Q / Lk

Chapter 5 of 5

Repeat, and keep the best tour

That was one iteration. Now the colony repeats it: build tours, evaporate, deposit. Edges of short tours keep getting stronger, so later ants follow them more often. The colony also remembers the best tour it has seen.
